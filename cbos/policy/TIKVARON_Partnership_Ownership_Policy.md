# TIKVARON Partnership & Ownership Policy

**Status:** Founder-approved operating principle, 16 November 2026  
**Scope:** All ventures, partnerships, collaborations, accelerators, investors, sponsors, suppliers, technology providers, knowledge partners, distributors and joint-development relationships.  
**Authority:** Founder approval is required for commitments. This is an internal governance framework, not legal advice.

## Central principle

> We build partnerships to strengthen TIKVARON, never to surrender its independence.

TIKVARON may partner for capability, knowledge, distribution, funding, technology and market access. It need not own every farm, employ every expert, build every model or operate every channel. It must retain control of product vision, customer relationships, core independently developed IP, quality standards, business model and the ability to operate independently.

## Ownership and independence rules

- No equity, options, warrants, voting rights, board rights or control rights without separate explicit founder approval.
- No compulsory joint venture, exclusivity, non-compete, right of first refusal, revenue share, guarantee, borrowing or minimum-spend obligation without a documented business case and separate approval.
- No hidden fees, uncapped liabilities, automatic renewals, punitive exit charges or lock-in by default.
- No unrestricted access to confidential information, credentials, customer/farmer records or source code.
- Programme invitation, introduction, verbal promise, grant discussion, accelerator admission or receipt of credits is not authority to sign or commit.

## Intellectual-property safeguards

- TIKVARON retains its brand, product vision, independently developed code, architecture, business methods, customer relationships and pre-existing IP.
- Each party's background IP must be identified in writing before work begins. A partner retains legitimate ownership of its pre-existing models, tools, infrastructure and materials.
- Any licence to TIKVARON IP must be purpose-limited, documented, non-exclusive by default and limited to what is necessary.
- Jointly developed deliverables require a written schedule defining ownership, licences, permitted reuse, derivative works, source-code access, maintenance, open-source obligations and exit rights before work starts. No implied assignment or joint ownership.
- No transfer or assignment of TIKVARON IP without separate explicit founder approval and independent legal review.

## Customer, data and confidentiality controls

Before sharing data, document purpose, lawful basis, data categories, controller/processor roles, access permissions, security, subprocessors, storage locations, cross-border transfer mechanism, retention, deletion/return, incident notification and audit rights. Share only the minimum necessary data. No partner may reuse customer or farmer data for unrelated purposes or claim ownership of TIKVARON's customer relationship.

## Partner evaluation scorecard

Score each dimension **0 (no evidence) to 5 (strong verified evidence)** and retain supporting evidence.

| Dimension | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Strategic fit | Direct contribution to an approved priority and measurable outcome |
| Capability and value | Specific expertise, technology, funding, distribution or commercial value |
| Commercial viability | Written fees, credits, co-funding, taxes, payment terms and future costs |
| Ownership and IP | Acceptable equity, IP, licence, derivative-work and control terms |
| Customer and data safety | Necessary and lawful access, security, privacy roles and retention |
| Execution credibility | Track record, capacity, references and accountable contacts |
| Exit and dependency | Portability, continuity, migration, termination and reasonable exit rights |

**Decision guidance:** 28–35 = strong candidate for formal review; 20–27 = conditional / negotiate; below 20 = defer or reject. A high score is not approval to sign. Any unresolved red-line ownership, control, data or legality issue overrides the score.

## Risk assessment

Record likelihood, impact, severity, evidence, mitigation, accountable owner and review date. Classify risks Low / Medium / High / Critical. Critical risks block approval until resolved.

Mandatory areas: ownership/control; IP assignment and licences; exclusivity and lock-in; data protection and cybersecurity; confidentiality and cross-border transfers; sanctions, beneficial ownership, export controls, anti-bribery and payment legality; reputation and financial health; delivery capacity; unclear pricing, tax, liability, indemnities and service levels; customer safety and regulated activity.

For Russia-linked or other elevated-risk counterparties, conduct enhanced entity, beneficial-owner, control, sanctions, banking, export-control and transaction screening before commercial engagement, data sharing or payment. Do not engage sanctioned parties or structure transactions to evade restrictions. If screening cannot be completed, put the prospect on hold.

## Agreement review checklist — mandatory before signature

- Verify legal identity, registration, beneficial ownership, authority to sign and references.
- Confirm purpose, scope, deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria, service levels and accountable owners.
- Confirm fees, taxes, payment milestones, credits, renewal costs, expenses and termination charges.
- Review equity, governance, exclusivity, non-compete, IP ownership, licences, derivative works and source-code terms.
- Review confidentiality, data protection, security, breach notification, audit and subcontracting.
- Review warranties, indemnities, liability caps, insurance, dispute resolution, governing law and jurisdiction.
- Define term, renewal, termination, cure periods, transition support, data return/deletion and post-termination rights.
- Attach final IP, data-processing, statement-of-work and security schedules.
- Obtain qualified legal/commercial review and record the exact final document version reviewed.
- Obtain explicit founder approval of the exact counterparty, document, scope, price and term before signing or clicking acceptance.

## Mandatory approval gates

0. **Research:** Public-source research only; no spend, commitments or confidential disclosure.
1. **Discovery:** Founder-approved outreach; non-binding discussion only; record recipient, date, message reference and response.
2. **Proposal / pilot:** Written scope, cohort, budget, deliverables, success measures, risks, data/IP terms and exit plan.
3. **Agreement:** Qualified legal/commercial review and explicit founder approval of the final version. No signature before both.
4. **Execution:** Signed terms, accountable owner, obligations calendar, risk controls, evidence and review cadence recorded.

Approval applies only to the exact final version and scope reviewed. Material changes require renewed approval. Silence, attendance, email discussion or programme admission is not consent.

## Central register requirements

Record organisation, country, relationship type, opportunity, status, official contact/source, outreach date and message reference, fit score/evidence, risk/likelihood/impact, mitigation, owner, next action/date, IP/data status, agreement status, founder approval evidence, legal review evidence and exit plan.

Allowed statuses: Proposed — not contacted; Inquiry sent — awaiting response; Due diligence; Negotiation; Approved — not yet active; Active; On hold; Rejected; Terminated.

A sent inquiry is not a partnership. A proposed prospect is not verified willingness to partner. Do not describe an organisation as a partner until the relationship is approved and documented.

## Founder decision rule

If a partnership makes TIKVARON stronger, more capable, more trusted and more commercially independent, explore it. If it requires TIKVARON to surrender ownership, control or its future unnecessarily, reject or renegotiate it.

**Automatic stop:** If a counterparty requires TIKVARON to surrender ownership or control as a condition of basic support, pause, document the clause and return it to the founder.
